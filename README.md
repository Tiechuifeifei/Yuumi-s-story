This piece is in memory of our cat, Yuumi.

The vermilion dandelion is me, the grey one is Yuumi, and the indigo one is my husband. At first there is only vermilion. Then Yuumi grows and leans in to say hello. Later, indigo appears, and Yuumi goes to greet him too. Threads slowly form between the three of us until we are all connected, turning and dancing together.

Then the wind comes. Yuumi comes apart and drifts away. He takes two seeds from each of us, and leaves two of his own behind with each of us. When the wind has passed, only a few threads remain between vermilion and indigo, still holding.

Every time the piece runs, the way we meet and the days we spend together are different. But the wind always rises at the same moment, and he always leaves then. Press and hold to try to keep him: the flowers draw close, the threads pull tight, he is held back for a moment, and he still goes. That is what I wanted to say. We can hold on, and we can not want to let go, but we cannot keep him.
